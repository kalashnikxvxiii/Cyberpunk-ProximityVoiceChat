VoiceChat @VERSION@ - two-player test
=====================================

Thanks for helping! This is a test build of VoiceChat, the proximity voice
chat for NightCoop. It takes about 30 minutes, together, in a co-op
session.

BEFORE YOU START
1. You need Cyberpunk 2077 with an up-to-date NightCoop and Cyber Engine
   Tweaks (CET).
2. Close the game and extract VoiceChat-@VERSION@.zip into the game folder
   (the one with "bin" and "red4ext").
3. Start the game and open the CET overlay: the guided setup asks for
   microphone, headphones and talk key (mouse 4 by default).
4. Both of you use this same zip.

The mod's menu is in CET > VoiceChat. The tabs you need are "General" and
"Diagnostics".

THE TESTS
One hosts the session, the other joins. For each item write down ok or not,
and a few words on what you heard. Voice starts only after the host has
pressed "Start" and the world has loaded: in the lobby the other player
shows as "(no VoiceChat)", and that's normal.

 1. Connection. General reads "host, 1 other players (1 with VoiceChat)"
    or "guest, ...". In Diagnostics, at the top, there is the other
    player's name.
 2. Take turns talking up close: you both hear each other well.
    The VoiceChat icon on screen lights up when someone talks.
 3. Distance. Walk away slowly, on foot: the voice drops quickly in the
    first 10-15 metres, then fades out around 50. Coming back it returns.
    Shop window: one inside a shop, the other outside in front of the
    window. The voice must arrive weak and dark. If it arrives clear, the
    one outside looks at the window and presses CET > VoiceChat >
    Environment > "What's in front of me? (collision groups)": take a
    screenshot of the list.
    Shout: in an open, quiet place walk away beyond 50 metres and shout:
    the other must hear you, faintly.
    Reverb: in a club with music, in a furnished room or in a street with
    traffic it must not be heard; in a concrete tunnel it must.
 4. Direction and walls. One talks behind a wall, then from another room
    with the door open: the voice comes from the right side and is muffled.
 5. Party. In CET > VoiceChat > Party both type the same party name and
    press "Create / join", then walk away beyond 20 metres: you still hear
    each other, like a radio. Press "Leave the party": from afar you no
    longer hear each other.
 6. Quality. Talk for a couple of minutes, then each copies the "Voice:"
    line from Diagnostics (a screenshot is fine too).
 7. Loading. One talks without stopping; the other fast travels or loads a
    save. Afterwards the voice must resume at once, with no delay and no
    old pieces.
 8. The one who joined leaves the session and joins again: the chat comes
    back by itself.
 9. Without the mod. The one who joined closes the game, moves the folder
    red4ext\plugins\VoiceChat out, joins the session again: the host must
    see "(no VoiceChat)" and co-op must work as always. Then put the folder
    back.
10. Mods turned off by the host. The host turns mods off for the session
    in NightCoop's settings ("sdk_session"): a warning appears in
    Diagnostics. Turned back on, the warning goes away and the voice
    returns.
11. If there are three of you: the two guests hear each other, each from
    the right side.
12. Quit the game from the menu: no crash.

If something goes wrong, look at the Diagnostics tab: it usually says what
happened and what to do. Write it down and carry on.

WHAT TO SEND AT THE END
- The list of items with ok or not, and the notes.
- The "Voice:" line from item 6.
- The log files, with the game closed:
  - <game folder>\red4ext\logs\voicechat-*.log (all of them)
  - NightCoop's logs (NightCoop's "logs" folder, the one with
    nightcoop-mods.txt)
  The logs contain the names of the session's players and what the mod
  did, not the audio. Feel free to read them before sending.

AFTER THE TEST
To remove the mod delete red4ext\plugins\VoiceChat and
bin\x64\plugins\cyber_engine_tweaks\mods\VoiceChat.
